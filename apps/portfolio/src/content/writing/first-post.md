## A second-level heading

Use paragraphs for the main rhythm of a post. Keep them focused, and let the
layout provide enough room around the words. Links can be [internal](/) or
[external](https://example.com).

### A smaller heading

You can emphasize something with **bold text**, use _italics_ for a quieter
shift in voice, or add `inline code` when writing about software.

> A blockquote is useful when another voice needs space of its own.

An unordered list:

- One thought.
- Another thought.
- A final thought with a little more detail.

An ordered list:

1. Start with the question.
2. Follow the useful thread.
3. Stop when the idea is clear.
